About me

Teacher, tutor and researcher.

I am an experienced secondary English teacher, private tutor and doctoral researcher at the University of Oxford. I have taught English from Key Stage 3 to A Level in secondary schools and have more than a decade of experience working one-to-one with students.

I have also worked as a Doctoral Teaching Fellow on the University of Oxford MSc in Education (Child Development). My background in both English and education means that my teaching is grounded in strong subject knowledge, but also in a detailed understanding of how students learn, develop confidence and respond to different kinds of support.

Lessons are carefully planned around the individual student. Depending on their needs, that might mean rebuilding foundations, developing more sophisticated analysis, practising exam technique, strengthening written expression or providing greater academic challenge.

Individual learning needs

Experienced support for EAL and SEND students.

I adapt teaching carefully to make learning accessible, while keeping expectations ambitious and subject content intellectually meaningful.

EAL

I have extensive experience supporting students for whom English is an additional language, including highly proficient learners studying English Language and Literature at an advanced level.

Tuition can focus on academic vocabulary, written precision, reading comprehension, literary terminology, essay structure, linguistic nuance and confidence in expressing complex ideas. I value each student's linguistic background and ensure that EAL students are challenged as subject learners, rather than treated simply as language beginners.

SEND

I work with students with a range of special educational needs and disabilities, including difficulties affecting reading, writing, attention, processing, organisation and confidence.

Lessons can be adapted through careful pacing, explicit modelling, chunked tasks, supported planning, visual organisation, repeated explanation and clear routines. I aim to reduce unnecessary barriers while preserving academic challenge, so that students can make meaningful progress and become increasingly independent.

Applications & admissions

Support can include personal statement development, refining academic interests, selecting and discussing wider reading, structuring evidence of subject engagement, interview preparation and developing the confident analytical thinking expected in highly selective admissions processes.

I have experience supporting Oxbridge applications and wider university applications. I have also served on the University of Oxford admissions panel for the MSc in Education, giving me direct experience of academic selection and admissions decision-making.
